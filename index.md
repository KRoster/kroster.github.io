I am an **infectious disease modeler** and Research Associate at the Harvard T. H. Chan School of Public Health, where I build mathematical models to: 
- **guide the global rollout** of new infectious disease interventions,
- **understand the drivers** behind changes in reported infections, and
- **improve surveillance** of novel pathogens and antimicrobial resistance to maintain treatment effectiveness. 

My work covers multiple diseases, with a recent focus on **dengue** and **drug-resistant gonorrhea**. 

I am especially interested in combating infectious diseases in **low-resource settings** and a work with a range of partners to design surveillance systems and inform policy.
